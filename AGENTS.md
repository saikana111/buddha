# Dialogue behavior to preserve

- Original source translations are the default answer to substantive questions,
  including follow-ups. The model selects relevant source IDs; it must not
  rewrite, shorten, or invent text labeled as a quotation.
- Choose by relevance and semantic scope, not question length. Open questions
  can receive substantial stories, dialogues, or extended teachings. Focused
  details can receive concise verses or precise passages. Do not force a story
  where no relevant story exists.
- Continue must finish unread browser pages, then ordered source chunks from
  the same edition before requesting new model prose. Preserve speaker,
  tradition, genre, and source attribution. Never silently turn narrator or
  disciple speech into Buddha speech.
- Follow-up retrieval should seek relevant unread sources. Generated
  explanation is a fallback after the current bounded source search yields no
  further suitable text; never claim that this exhausts the entire canon.
  Keep generated explanation distinct from quotation.
- Preserve player-specific reading progress and source history across reloads.
  New topics start a new source search. Identity and casual conversation remain
  natural and need not be forced into quotations.
- Every pending reply needs cancellation, a finite request deadline, and a
  guaranteed composer unlock on failure. Continue through stored text should
  not depend on model availability.
- Keep the interface minimal: scenery, dialogue, composer, and contextual
  controls. The first-meeting appearance plays once per player profile.
- Frame the scene's character as an unnamed wise teacher, not definitively the
  historical Buddha. During synthesis, show restrained narrative waiting text
  about the seated man considering the question. Never mislabel invented
  narration as a canonical quotation; keep identity answers honest when asked.
