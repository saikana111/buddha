# Meditation audio

The active bell is a real recording, played at its original pitch, not the earlier synthesized 196 Hz bell. No universal Buddhist frequency or therapeutic frequency claim is made.

The Ogg recordings are stored unchanged. Runtime playback adjusts gain and applies fades. The ambient arrangement combines sparse bowl playback, an original synthesized flute-like pentatonic phrase and filtered-noise breeze. This arrangement is artistic accompaniment, not a reconstructed temple ceremony or canonical prescription. No endorsement by the recording authors is implied.
